HOW TO UPLOAD THIS PAIR  (CSV statement + CSV general ledger)
============================================================

The bank processed the Stellar Parts ACH ($4,250) TWICE (May 10 & 11); the GL booked it once.

In the Streamlit sidebar -> 'Upload statement':
  1. Bank statement box  ->  bank_statement.csv
  2. GL export box       ->  gl_export.csv     <-- enables matching!
  3. Account ID          ->  UPL-CSV-DUP
  4. Currency / Period   ->  USD / 2026-05
  5. Leave 'Auto-detect opening/closing' ON (opening 30,000.00, closing 32,100.00 read from the file)
  6. Click 'Upload & run'.

EXPECTED RESULT:
  75% match -> ESCALATED: the Stellar Parts ACH was charged twice; the duplicate is detected and a reversal journal entry is suggested.

Upload the statement WITHOUT gl_export.csv and you get 0% (statement-only analysis - nothing to match against).
