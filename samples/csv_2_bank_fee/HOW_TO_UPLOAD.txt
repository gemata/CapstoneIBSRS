HOW TO UPLOAD THIS PAIR  (CSV statement + CSV general ledger)
============================================================

3 of 4 bank lines match the GL. The bank's $45 monthly service charge was never booked in the ledger.

In the Streamlit sidebar -> 'Upload statement':
  1. Bank statement box  ->  bank_statement.csv
  2. GL export box       ->  gl_export.csv     <-- enables matching!
  3. Account ID          ->  UPL-CSV-FEE
  4. Currency / Period   ->  USD / 2026-05
  5. Leave 'Auto-detect opening/closing' ON (opening 20,000.00, closing 25,355.00 read from the file)
  6. Click 'Upload & run'.

EXPECTED RESULT:
  75% match -> CLOSED_WITH_ADJUSTMENTS: the $45 bank fee (not in the GL) becomes an auto journal entry.

Upload the statement WITHOUT gl_export.csv and you get 0% (statement-only analysis - nothing to match against).
