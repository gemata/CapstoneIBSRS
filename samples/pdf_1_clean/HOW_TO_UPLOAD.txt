HOW TO UPLOAD THIS PAIR  (PDF statement + CSV general ledger)
============================================================

Clean account: all 3 bank lines are recorded in the GL (same ref+amount).

In the Streamlit sidebar -> 'Upload statement':
  1. Bank statement box  ->  bank_statement.pdf
  2. GL export box       ->  gl_export.csv     <-- enables matching!
  3. Account ID          ->  UPL-PDF-CLEAN
  4. Currency / Period   ->  USD / 2026-05
  5. Leave 'Auto-detect opening/closing' ON (opening 16,000.00, closing 19,200.00 read from the file)
  6. Click 'Upload & run'.

EXPECTED RESULT:
  100% bank match rate, 0 exceptions -> CLOSED_CLEAN (confirm & close).

Upload the statement WITHOUT gl_export.csv and you get 0% (statement-only analysis - nothing to match against).
